The INBRND framework

Five connected parts, clockwise from the top: Positioning, Narrative, Platforms, Opportunities, Signals, and back to Positioning. The outer ring represents cumulative authority, recognition, and career momentum, an outcome of the system.

As authority and recognition compound, career momentum grows.

  1. Positioning

    What do you want to be known for?

    Your strategic lane, not your job title.

  2. Narrative

    How does your experience connect?

    A clear story makes you easier to understand.

  3. Platforms

    Where does your story live?

    The places where people encounter your work, thinking, and perspective.

  4. Opportunities

    What becomes possible when the system works?

    New conversations, relationships, roles, clients, and collaborations.

  5. Signals

    What gives your positioning credibility?

    Projects, writing, talks, awards, partnerships, recommendations, and visible contributions.
