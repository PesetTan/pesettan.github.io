# Shared Roots

A simple lotus-inspired family symbol. Two outer petals represent the Cambodian and Chinese sides of the family, meeting in one shared center. The open curved base suggests the Bay and a new home built by children of immigrants. These are personal design meanings, not a claim to a traditional cultural emblem.

The header uses a white outline against the existing blue landscape. The favicon uses the same silhouette in deep green on a pale background. The transparent reusable vector is `family/assets/family-symbol.svg`.

Keep the mark upright, retain its open space, and use one color. No flags, lettering, or added ornament is required.
